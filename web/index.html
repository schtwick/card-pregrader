Card Pre-Grader

Should this card go in for grading?

Take a photo of the card. This phone measures the centering, checks the edges and corners for wear, and estimates a PSA, BGS and CGC grade. Your photos stay on this phone.

Getting the grader ready…
How to take a good photo
  • Take the card out of its sleeve or toploader.
  • Lay it flat on something plain and dark (a black mousepad or black paper). For a black-bordered card, use a light grey surface.
  • Use bright, even light, like daylight near a window. No flash, no shadows across the card.
  • Hold the phone straight above the card and get close: the card should fill most of the picture.
  • Tap the card on screen to focus before you take the picture.
  • Optional, after grading: add two angled-light photos to check the surface for scratches and dents (instructions appear with the results).

Centering

Green lines = the card's edges. Pink lines = where the border meets the picture. Red boxes = edge wear; yellow boxes = check by eye; dashed blue boxes = surface damage.

Pick a line, then drag it on the card or use the arrows. The loupe shows a close-up while you drag.

Surface check (scratches, dents, creases)

Surface damage only shows when light skims across the card. Add two extra photos of this side:

  1. Leave the card exactly where it is. Turn off the room lights.
  2. Hold a lamp or a second phone's flashlight low on the left, about a hand's width above the table, so the light skims across the card. Take a photo from straight above, like before.
  3. Move the light low to the top of the card and take another.
